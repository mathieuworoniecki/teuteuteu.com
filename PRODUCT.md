# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Public web visitors discovering or revisiting the historical teuteuteu.com experience on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Preserve the absurd, minimal Flash-era interaction with its original sound, button and synchronized shakes, while adding a global click counter and a discreet way to support hosting costs.

## Brand Personality

Absurd, nostalgic, minimal.

## Anti-references

Modern SaaS landing pages, decorative cards, glass effects, marketing copy, dashboards and any interface that competes with the single blue button.

## Design Principles

- Preserve original assets and timing before interpreting them.
- Keep the empty white page as part of the experience.
- Make modern additions visually subordinate to the original button.
- Prefer one obvious interaction over explanatory interface elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard controls and explicit accessible states. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep readable contrast and never require sound or motion to understand the controls.
